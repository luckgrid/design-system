## The PR's review questions

1. **Is the pattern boundary generic enough?** Yes for the P0 set: selectable list, pane navigation, status footer and confirmation each name a second consumer, and Prompt-TUI's fields stay consumer-owned.
2. **Does theme projection avoid duplicated tokens?** Yes as written: role identity plus a checked table on the Tailwind precedent, with resolved values as derivative fixtures. TR-06 adds a caution against copying Opaline's flat output.
3. **Documentation, helpers, headless controllers or widgets?** Still open (decision register question 2). GPUI Kit's published `gpui-base` and ratcn's split are the two concrete data points.
4. **Are references to other workspace plans scoped?** Now yes, after TR-07.
5. **Does the plan justify a separate experimental PR?** Yes: M1 and M2 are bounded, and nothing here authorizes a crate or a CSS change.
